🐾 Cat Fun Facts 🐾

  • Cats see better than people in low light, but they still need some light to see.
  • A cat’s whiskers help it sense nearby objects and judge whether it can fit through a gap.
  • Cats see a more limited range of colors than humans do.
  • Cats are most active around dawn and dusk. This is called being crepuscular.
  • Many cats sleep for around 12–18 hours a day.
  • A cat can turn each ear toward a different sound, and its ears can rotate widely.
  • Each cat ear has 32 muscles.
  • A cat’s ears can help show how it is feeling. Flattened ears can mean it feels scared or stressed.
  • Most cats have 24 whiskers on their cheeks, usually 12 on each side.
  • Whiskers can sense tiny movements in the air.
  • Whiskers help cats judge whether a narrow opening is wide enough to pass through.
  • Cats have scent glands around their cheeks and paws.
  • When cats scratch, they leave both visible marks and scent signals.
  • A purr often means a cat is content, but cats may also purr when stressed or in pain.
  • Kittens are born with their eyes and ear canals closed; these open as they grow.
  • Cats use scent and body language to communicate with other cats.
  • Many pet cats use meows especially to communicate with people.
  • Cats have a reflective layer in their eyes that helps them see in dim light.
  • Cats have fewer types of color-sensing cone cells than people do.

KATS! — SERVER RULES

Welcome to KATS! Please read and follow all the rules to keep our community friendly and welcoming.

05. RESPECT PRIVACY

06. USE CHANNELS CORRECTLY

10. NO IMPERSONATION
