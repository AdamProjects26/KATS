KATS! — SERVER RULES

Welcome to KATS! Please read and follow all the rules to keep our community friendly and welcoming.

01. RESPECT EVERYONE

Treat all members with respect. Bullying, harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

02. NO SPAM

Do not spam messages, emojis, stickers, reactions or repeatedly ping members.

03. NO NSFW CONTENT

Keep all conversations and media appropriate for a general audience. No NSFW, explicit or inappropriate content.

04. NO DRAMA

Avoid starting arguments, provoking members or bringing unnecessary drama into the community.

05. RESPECT PRIVACY

Never share someone's personal information, private messages or photos without their permission.

06. USE CHANNELS CORRECTLY

Keep conversations in their appropriate channels. Check channel descriptions before posting.

07. NO BEGGING

Do not repeatedly beg for roles, promotions, permissions, Nitro or other benefits.

08. LISTEN TO STAFF

Follow reasonable instructions from moderators and administrators. Do not argue with staff in public channels.

09. NO MALICIOUS LINKS

Do not share suspicious links, scams, phishing attempts or harmful files.

10. NO IMPERSONATION

Do not impersonate other members, staff or public figures to mislead others.

11. USE COMMON SENSE

Not everything can be covered by these rules. Be considerate and use your best judgement.

MODERATION & CONSEQUENCES

Breaking the rules may result in:

  • Verbal warning
  • Official warning
  • Timeout
  • Kick
  • Temporary ban
  • Permanent ban

Staff reserve the right to take appropriate action depending on the severity of the situation.

By being a member in KATS, you agree to follow these rules.
